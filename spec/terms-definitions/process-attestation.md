[[def: process attestation, process attestation, process attestations]]

~ An [[ref: attestation]], made by the party that captured the production of a digital artifact, that the artifact was produced through a recorded process. It is bound to the artifact's digest and to a fresh challenge supplied by the party asking, so that it answers one question from one party at one time. It refers to an [[ref: attestation receipt]] rather than restating it; the receipt is appraised under its own specification, and the process attestation establishes who answered, about which artifact, and when.

~ See also: [[ref: attestation receipt]], [[ref: determination]], [[ref: context collapse]], [[ref: holder]], [[ref: verifier]].
