[[def: determination, determination, determinations]]

~ The conclusion an appraisal reaches about an [[ref: attestation receipt]], expressed in the vocabulary of the specification that defines the receipt (for example `authentic`, `inconclusive`, `suspicious` or `invalid`). A determination states what the evidence supports; it is not a statement that something is true, and the party relying on it decides what weight it bears. Determinations are not normalized across specifications: one means only what its own specification defines it to mean.

~ See also: [[ref: process attestation]], [[ref: attestation receipt]].
