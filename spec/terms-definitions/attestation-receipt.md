[[def: attestation receipt, attestation receipt, attestation receipts]]

~ The evidence object a [[ref: process attestation]] refers to: the record a capture system produced while a digital artifact was being made, such as a signed evidence packet or a content-credentials manifest. A [[ref: verifier]] appraises a receipt under the specification that defines it; the process attestation names the receipt by location and digest and does not vouch for its contents.

~ See also: [[ref: process attestation]], [[ref: determination]].

~ Source: [ToIP Trust Tasks registry, process-attestation/0.1](https://trusttasks.org/spec/process-attestation/0.1).
