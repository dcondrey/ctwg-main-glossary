[[def: context collapse, context collapse]]

~ The loss of the circumstances that gave a piece of evidence its meaning when it is moved out of the exchange that produced it. A [[ref: determination]] read without the challenge, the artifact and the parties it answered carries no more weight than an unsigned claim. A [[ref: process attestation]] prevents it by binding each response to the request it answers, so a response retained as evidence still shows what it was evidence of.

~ See also: [[ref: process attestation]], [[ref: determination]].

~ Source: ToIP white paper, *Process Attestation as Context-Bound Evidence* (draft, 2026).
